A · Two tabs

Following and Browse as tabs on one screen. Following shows each wiki as a card with its languages as removable chips. Clear separation, one tap to swap views, and the two lists never compete for space.

C · Drill-down

The screen you land on is only what you follow — compact and calm. Browsing is a separate screen behind +, and languages a screen behind that. Scales to any catalogue size, at the cost of one more tap to add.

Common to all three: search matches both the autonym and the English name, so "Japanese" and "日本語" both find ja. Following a wiki with no languages chosen is treated as not following it. Changing the selection resets the feed.
